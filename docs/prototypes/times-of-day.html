For your review

Decisions

  1. When the sky changes

    I propose the new phase arrives with the minute change: the avalanche runs and the new sky is in place when the skiers drop in. The alternative is a gradual blend across the two-hour window, which costs more colours and more redraws.

  2. No location yet

    Until the phone sends today's times, the watch uses the last times it received. On first install it falls back to 07:00 and 19:00.

  3. Setting modes

    The proposal has three modes: Follow sunrise and sunset (default), Fixed times and Always midday. Keep all three?

  4. Campfire flicker

    The fire flickers only while the face is redrawing: during a minute change, and every second when the seconds complication is on. Otherwise it holds still, to save battery.

  5. Night snow

    At night the snow turns moonlit blue, with dark blue complication text. Check that the digits still read well, or keep the snow white.

  6. Palette budget

    The watch stores the background in 16 colours. Every phase fits; each card shows its count.
