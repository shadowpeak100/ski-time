Weather

The sky shows the current weather from the forecast the watch already uses for temperature. Every weather layer takes its colours from the time of day, so a cloud at sunset glows orange and a cloud at night is a dark blue shape. Pick any weather and time below, or tap a cell in the grid.

What each code shows

ClearWMO 0

Today's sky: sun or moon and stars.

Partly cloudyWMO 1–2

Flat, fluffy clouds: soft bumps on top and a flat base, sitting behind the peaks. The sun still shows.

OvercastWMO 3

A two-layer cloud deck and a flatter sky. No sun, moon or stars.

FogWMO 45–48

Cloud deck plus a haze that swallows the peaks and fades out above the digits.

DrizzleWMO 51–57

Light, short streaks under an overcast sky. Freezing drizzle looks the same.

RainWMO 61–67, 80–82

Longer, faster streaks slanting with the wind. Showers and freezing rain are included.

SnowWMO 71–77, 85–86

Drifting flakes, some two pixels wide, each with a tiny shadow so they show against the snow.

ThunderstormWMO 95–99

Dark storm clouds, heavy rain, and one lightning bolt between the two big peaks on the left. It flashes every 5 seconds as a double flicker, with a flash of the sky. Hail is included.

For your review

Decisions

  1. Rain and snow at rest

    Falling rain means redrawing the watch many times a second, which costs battery. I propose it falls while the face is already redrawing, during a minute change and every second with the seconds complication. The rest of the time it holds still, the way the campfire does.

  2. Lightning

    A bolt and a flash roughly every 6 seconds while the face is animating, and at least once at every minute change during a storm.

  3. Wind

    The forecast also gives wind direction. I propose rain and snow slant with the wind, left or right. The preview slants left.

  4. Basalt

    Basalt has almost no memory left. I propose weather runs on Emery only, like the floodlights and trees.

  5. Setting

    A Show weather switch in settings, on by default. When it's off, the sky always shows clear.

  6. How often

    The weather refreshes with the temperature, every 30 minutes, and a new sky arrives with the next minute change.
