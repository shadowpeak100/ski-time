Battery modes

A new setting chooses how much the face animates. Constant rain or snow means redrawing the watch many times a second, which drains the battery. The still versions show the same weather without redrawing.

Most motion

Full animations

May use more battery.

  • Rain, drizzle and snow fall all the time.
  • Lightning strikes every few seconds with a sky flash.
  • Minute changes play the full ski out, avalanche and draw.
Default

Balanced

Motion only when the time changes.

  • Minute changes play the full animation.
  • Between them, raindrops and snowflakes hold still.
  • A storm shows one lightning bolt fixed in the sky.
Least motion

Battery saver

The same as today's saver.

  • The time changes instantly, with no ski out or avalanche.
  • Weather is shown still, as in Balanced.
  • The seconds complication shows a dash.

Weather held still

Drizzle

Short drops held in place. Twice as many as the falling version, so it reads without motion.

Rain

Longer slanted drops held in place, with more of them than the falling version.

Thunderstorm

Dark storm clouds, heavy rain held in place, and one lightning bolt between the two big peaks on the left. The bolt follows natural lightning: a thin pale core with a soft blue glow, many small irregular kinks, and short branches that split off and fade.

Still snow · your pick

Snowflakes

When held still, the face shows half again as many flakes as when they fall, so it reads as snowing without motion.

D · Crystals and blue

Big flakes are small plus-shaped crystals: a white centre, pale blue arms and a dark blue shadow. Small flakes are pale blue dots. The same look is used at every time of day.
